# Interpretability guide

PRISM-CalNet exports:

- t-SNE colored separately by disease and acquisition origin;
- frozen unseen-database input×gradient spectral saliency;
- quality-stratified AUROC;
- risk–coverage curves;
- clinical-category error taxonomy;
- hard-negative COPD probability margins;
- acquisition-domain probe diagnostics before manuscript claims are made.

Do not claim that t-SNE proves domain invariance. Use it only as descriptive evidence. Domain-invariance statements require quantitative probe reduction and strict LODO results.
